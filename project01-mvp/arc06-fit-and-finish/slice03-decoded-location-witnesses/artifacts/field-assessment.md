## Population implications

The raw route preserved 3,243 discovery records and both base contexts without typed-model projection. This sample contains 2,362 string and 881 integer `DD.UA` values; string/integer hex widths span 11 through 14 digits. All discovery integers are within JavaScript's safe range here, and the 19 integer base scalars are also within that range. Base scalars have 12/13/14 digit representations and string/integer kinds. This does not establish behavior for zero, negative, over-width, unsafe numeric, malformed, or missing scalar values.

BaseContext has 49 HomePlanetBase, one FreighterBase and four PlayerShipBase records. Its 207 endpoints include 74 `Base` entries and 133 incompatible endpoint types. The strict relationship predicate accounts for all 49/74 eligible records, all 44 accepted edges, five unmatched bases, 30 unmatched endpoints, zero ambiguity, and zero field-eligibility exclusions; incompatible type ordinals are also retained. The all-base preliminary “one ambiguity” is superseded by the fully rechecked strict rule, whose result is 44 unique edges and no ambiguity. Both contexts' native current/previous `UniverseAddress` wrappers are objects. The alternate `UniversalAddress` and `PreviousUniversalAddress` wrappers are absent in both contexts; `UniversalAddress` is absent on all 224 context endpoints. Their wrapper/member populations remain separately counted in the unchanged `population.json`.

## Bounded design ready for CDC consideration

CDC can consider a field-specific, loss-preserving `BaseContext` base-address adapter that accepts the observed raw string/integer representations, decodes components without passing integer input through an unsafe floating-point conversion, validates supported width/range before yielding an address, and retains the original field context. The basis is the 44 independently paired Base endpoint comparisons plus the scalar kind/width/range population. The adapter would be a proposal only; this slice neither changes code nor decides unsupported-input behavior.

A separate discovery adapter is not supported by these relationships. The 42 scalar equalities and candidate-derived reality counts are insufficient linkage. The source-specific NMSCoordinates path and Companion candidate both match the independent expanded values on these 44 tested base fields. CDC may consider that corroboration alongside their exact source bodies and caller applicability; this evidence does not select either implementation, establish other field families, or determine invalid-input and compatibility behavior.
