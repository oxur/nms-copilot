## NMSCoordinates literal behavior and diagnostic results

The base decoder's field path is PersistentBases -> HexToAll -> GetGalacticCoordHex -> GalacticToPortal. Its documented candidate layout and actual right-suffix slices agree for full-width inputs. HexToAll does not validate canonical spelling before slicing:

- With 0x, it converts using hexadecimal Convert.ToInt64; without that prefix it first parses a signed decimal Int64, then emits unpadded hex. Unprefixed leading zeros therefore do not preserve a hex spelling.
- Fixed Substring offsets require at least 16 characters including the prefix. 0x123 parses and then fails at slicing; 0xGG000000000000 fails at conversion. A bare 00000000000000 is decimal zero, becomes 0x0, then fails at slicing. The full-width 0x00000000000000 succeeds as zero.
- A bare -1 converts to 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF; the selected 3-character planet slice is FFF (4095), not a four-bit planet. A bare decimal spelling of u64::MAX exceeds signed Int64 and fails conversion. A prefixed 16-digit hex all-ones spelling follows the signed-hex conversion route; field slicing again yields planet FFF.
- GalacticToPortal concatenates the Planet argument as decimal text. Direct sentinels W-PLANET-A and W-PLANET-F produce 13-character outputs with prefixes 10 and 15; this is a source formatting defect/quirk, not an intended portal code.
- GetGalacticCoordHex recognizes colon form only at length 19 or compact form at length 16; unmatched shapes return a default zero-valued coordinate object. Its caller-generated colon form is length 19.

These are transcribed results checked against complete pinned method bodies; the original C# executable was not run. They describe NMSCoordinates literal behavior on representational diagnostics, not target product policy. Complete inputs, outputs, failures, and method pins are recorded in witnesses.json.
