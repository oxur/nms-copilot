## Grouped value, dependency and maintenance findings

**Identity, cache and source truth.** S05–08 lay down the cache compatibility envelope, galaxy-qualified key and galaxy-scoped query/routing foundations. S15–19 add full update ownership, cache write-through, normal REPL composition, placeholder provenance and unknown-system restarts. S20–23 add validated source-address decoding, whole-save caller migration, explicit discovery-name observations and application.
