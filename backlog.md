## Convention

Enduring discovery notes live in `backlog/<slug>/README.md`, indexed here. Duncan proposed this category to preserve discussion before project planning, then requested that these notes be committed and pushed on `main`. Additional files within a topic should serve an actual reading, evidence or access need.

These directories preserve ideas without assigning project numbers, commissioning research or changing project acceptance criteria. When a topic gains an approved delivery home, link its plan and retain the original discussion and evidence distinctions. Project/arc/slice plans and ledgers continue to live in the separate `planning` worktree; this index does not replace that roadmap.
